FAQ

Questions people ask first

Does the AI replace a doctor?

No. The AI assistant collects your symptoms and prepares the case. A licensed doctor reviews it, makes the diagnosis and issues any prescription or referral.

How do I get an account?

Through one of our fintech partners. When your bank, wallet or insurance app activates EZaid, you receive an SMS with a personal link. There is no separate registration on this site.

I tried to sign in and my account was not found. Why?

Accounts exist only for people whose partner has activated EZaid. Use the phone number your partner has on file, or open the link from your welcome SMS.

Who are the doctors?

General practitioners, pediatricians and specialists licensed to practise in your country.

What can a doctor send me?

Medical guidance, a prescription, a referral for lab tests or other examinations, or a referral to a specialist. Each document is a PDF you can download from your case.

How much does it cost?

EZaid is part of the subscription you have with our partner. Check your partner's app for the plan details.

About us

Good care shouldn't depend on where you live or what time it is

A child's fever at 2 a.m. A cough that has lasted a week too long. A question you are not sure is worth a doctor's time. For millions of people, getting an answer means a day off work, a long trip, or a waiting list. Many simply go without.

EZaid exists to close that gap. We believe everyone deserves a doctor's attention when they need it: quickly, affordably, and in their own language.

Our mission

To make a doctor's care as easy to reach as a message.

We do it by letting each side do what it does best. Our AI assistant is patient and always awake. It listens to your story, asks the questions a good clinician would ask, checks for warning signs, and prepares a clear summary. Then a licensed doctor takes over, reviews the case, and makes the decision: the diagnosis, the prescription, the referral.

AI that aids. Doctors who treat.

That is the whole idea, and we will not blur the line between the two.

What we believe

Doctors decide

Technology can prepare, organize, and speed things up. It should never have the final word on someone's health. Every diagnosis and every prescription on EZaid is signed by a licensed physician.

Time is part of care

A doctor who spends less time on paperwork has more time for patients. A patient who gets an answer in minutes worries less and acts sooner.

Access should be simple

EZaid comes through the banking and wallet apps people already use and trust. No new app to hunt for and no complicated sign-up: a text message, a link, and you are in.

Care is local

Health is personal, and so is the way people talk about it. We work with local doctors, in local languages, under local rules, in every country we serve.

Trust is earned

Your health information belongs to you. We protect it, we never sell it, and our partners never see it.

What EZaid looks like in real life

The stories below are illustrative examples of how the service is designed to work.

A fever at midnight

Maria's four-year-old wakes up hot and crying. Instead of deciding alone whether to wait until morning, she opens EZaid and describes what she sees. The assistant asks about breathing, fluids, and how long the fever has lasted, then passes the case to a pediatrician. Within minutes the doctor confirms it is safe to treat at home, explains what to watch for, and sends a prescription. Maria goes back to bed knowing what to do.

The appointment he kept putting off

Rahul drives twelve hours a day and has ignored his headaches for months, because a clinic visit means a lost day of income. During a break he answers the assistant's questions from his phone. A doctor reviews the summary, orders a blood pressure check and a lab test, and follows up when the results come in. A problem that could have grown quietly is caught early.

Help in her own language

Thandi's mother is 71 and uncomfortable explaining her symptoms in English. With EZaid she describes them in isiZulu, at her own pace, without feeling rushed. The doctor receives a clear summary and replies with guidance and a referral to a specialist. Her daughter keeps every document in one place for the next visit.

1. Who we are

EZaid Inc., a Delaware corporation ("EZaid", "we", "us"), operates the EZaid telemedicine service. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it and what choices you have. It applies to this website, the EZaid web portal and any related apps and phone lines.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, phone number and, if you add it, your email address. We first receive your name and phone number from the partner that activates EZaid for you.
  • Health information: what you tell the AI assistant and doctors, your medical history, allergies and medicines, and the diagnoses, prescriptions and referrals issued to you.
  • Messages to us: what you send through the contact form, by email or by phone.
  • Technical information: device and browser type, IP address, and sign-in and usage logs needed to run and secure the service.

3. How we use it

  • To provide care: run your AI consultation, prepare the case summary and connect you with a doctor.
  • To manage your account: verify your phone number, send one-time codes and service notifications.
  • To keep the service safe: prevent misuse, keep clinical records and audit decisions.
  • To improve the service, using information that has been de-identified wherever possible.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

We process health information with your consent or on another basis the law allows for providing healthcare. We do not sell your personal information and we do not use your health information for advertising.

HIPAA. We handle health information in line with the U.S. Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) and with the health-privacy laws of the other countries where we operate. We use and disclose protected health information only as those laws allow: to provide care, to run the service, and where the law requires it.

4. How AI is used

Your conversation with the AI assistant is processed by AI models, including models supplied by specialist providers who act on our instructions under contract. The AI prepares information for a doctor. A licensed doctor, not the AI, makes every diagnosis and prescribing decision.

5. Who we share it with

  • Doctors who handle your case.
  • Service providers who host our systems, deliver SMS messages and supply AI and communication tools. They may use your information only to provide services to us. Providers that handle protected health information do so under written agreements, including business associate agreements where HIPAA requires them.
  • Our partners receive only what they need to manage your subscription, such as whether your account is active. We never share your medical information with them.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.

6. Where your information is stored

We store and process information in data centres that may be located outside your country. When we transfer information across borders, we use the safeguards required by the law that applies to you.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is open. Medical records are kept for as long as healthcare record-keeping laws in your country require. After that we delete or de-identify them.

8. Security

Your conversations and documents are protected with end-to-end encryption, from your device to your doctor, and all information is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access to medical information is limited to the doctors handling your case and to authorised staff, and every access is logged. Our safeguards follow the HIPAA Security Rule. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your sign-in details private and tell us at once if you suspect unauthorised access. If a breach affects your information, we will notify you as the law requires.
